PegDown is your digital motorcycle companion for tracking lean angles, acceleration, and braking forces in real-time.

1. Main Screen
Shows your current lean angle and G-forces.
- Colors: Green (easy), Yellow (sporty), Red (limit).
- Markers: Yellow shows the last corner's peak, Blue shows the tour's maximum.
- Self-Correction: The app automatically adjusts the zero-point if you ride straight for a while (>40km/h).

2. Setup & Display
- Calibration: Tap the gauge to set 0° manually.
- Battery Saving: Optimized for OLED screens (uses true black to save power).

3. Recording in the Background
- Multi-Tasking: Recording continues even if you switch to a navigation app or turn off the screen. An icon in the status bar shows that PegDown is active.
- Lock View: Keeps the screen from rotating while you lean.

4. Automatic Start
- Manual: Tap Record to start/stop.
- Auto-Idle: Long-press Record. It will "wake up" and start tracking as soon as you reach your set speed.

5. Exporting Data
- Formats: Save as GPX (for maps/Calimoto) or CSV (for Excel).
- Smart Files: Redundant data is removed to keep files small without losing precision.

Safety first: Never operate the app while riding!
